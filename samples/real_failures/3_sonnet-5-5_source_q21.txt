In fiscal 2025 (year ended June 30, 2025), Microsoft reported revenue of $281,724 million (about $281.7 billion), gross profit, which the filing labels "gross margin," of $193,893 million (about $193.9 billion), and operating income of $128,528 million (about $128.5 billion). That works out to a gross margin of roughly 68.8% ($193,893 / $281,724) and an operating margin of roughly 45.6% ($128,528 / $281,724). For comparison, fiscal 2024 had a gross margin of about 69.8% ($171,008 / $245,122) and an operating margin of about 44.6% ($109,433 / $245,122), so gross margin slipped by about one percentage point while operating margin improved by about one point.
