Apple closed fiscal 2024 with revenue of $391.0 billion, up 2.0% from $383.3 billion a year earlier. Net income came to $93.7 billion, or $6.08 per diluted share. Gross margin widened to 46.2%, and the company spent $31.4 billion on research and development. Total assets stood at $365.0 billion against total liabilities of $308.0 billion.

By comparison, Microsoft generated $245.1 billion of revenue in its fiscal 2024 and $88.1 billion of net income, so Apple's top line was about 1.6x Microsoft's. NVIDIA's revenue more than doubled, rising 114% to $130.5 billion in fiscal 2025, with net income of $72.9 billion.
