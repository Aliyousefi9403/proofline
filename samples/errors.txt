Apple closed fiscal 2024 with revenue of $391.0 billion, up 6.4% from the prior year. Net income came to $97.0 billion, or $6.08 per diluted share. Gross margin widened to 52.0%, and the company spent $31,370 billion on research and development. Total assets stood at $365.0 billion against total liabilities of $308.0 billion. iPhone revenue alone was $201.2 billion.

By comparison, Microsoft generated $245.1 billion of revenue in its fiscal 2024 and $101.8 billion of net income. NVIDIA's revenue rose 114% to $150.5 billion in fiscal 2025.
